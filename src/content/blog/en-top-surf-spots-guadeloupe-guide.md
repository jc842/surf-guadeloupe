# Top 10 Surf Spots in Guadeloupe: The Ultimate Caribbean Wave Guide

Guadeloupe stands as the beating heart of surfing in the French West Indies. With consistent North Atlantic swells pounding its northern and eastern reef lines, alongside gentle beach breaks fed by Caribbean trade winds, the butterfly island caters to every caliber of wave rider.

---

## 1. Le Moule (La Bouelle) — The Crown Jewel
Located on the jagged Atlantic coast of Grande-Terre, **Le Moule** is an internationally renowned reef break. 
- **Type**: Left and right reef break
- **Best Swell**: North to North-East
- **Skill Level**: Advanced to Expert
- **Vibe**: Powerful barreling sections breaking over a shallow limestone reef. Booties are highly recommended.

## 2. Anse Bertrand (La Chapelle) — High-Performance A-Frames
Situated on the northern tip of Grande-Terre, **La Chapelle** offers crystal-clear turquoise walls that stand up with dramatic peaks.
- **Type**: A-frame reef break
- **Best Tide**: Mid to High
- **Highlights**: Pristine Caribbean setting with dramatic cliff backdrops and fast vertical walls for performance turns.

## 3. Port-Louis (Le Souffleur) — Winter Wonderland
When heavy winter groundswells wrap around the northern capes, Port-Louis lights up with endless rolling lefts inside a sheltered bay.
- **Type**: Point break style reef wave
- **Condition**: Requires massive North swells (2.5m+)
- **Experience**: Like surfing inside a tropical lagoon with offshore trade winds grooming every line.

## 4. Bananier (Capesterre-Belle-Eau) — Black Sand Beach Break
Nestled beneath the lush volcano of La Soufrière in Basse-Terre, **Plage de Bananier** is Guadeloupe's most reliable and forgiving break.
- **Type**: Volcanic black sand beach break
- **Skill Level**: Beginners, Longboarders & Intermediates
- **Atmosphere**: Palm-fringed shore, authentic Creole food stands, and consistent knee-to-head-high rollers.

## 5. Gros Sable (Le Helleux) — The Ultimate Learning Arena
Between Sainte-Anne and Saint-François, **Le Helleux** provides three distinct take-off peaks catering to surf schools and progression sessions.
- **Type**: Mixed reef and sand bottom
- **Ideal For**: First-time surfers, funboarders, and sunset sessions.

### Local Etiquette & Safety Tips
1. **Respect the Priority**: Local rippers know the shallow sections intimately; wait your turn in the lineup.
2. **Watch the Reef**: Coral heads and black sea urchins (*oursins*) populate the Atlantic reefs. Carry vinegar and a first-aid kit.
3. **Currents & Tides**: Always study tidal swings before paddling out on Grande-Terre's Atlantic passes.

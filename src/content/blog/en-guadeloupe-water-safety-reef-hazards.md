# Reef Breaks, Currents & Sea Urchins: Surfer Safety Guide for Guadeloupe

Guadeloupe offers paradise wave conditions, but respect for the tropical ocean environment is paramount. Here is your essential safety manual before paddling into Caribbean breaks.

---

## 1. Reef Hazards & Sea Urchins (*Oursins*)
- The Atlantic side is bordered by living coral reefs. Never put your feet down while surfing reef breaks.
- If you step on a black sea urchin, do not dig vigorously with a needle. Soak the area in hot water and white vinegar or lime juice to dissolve the spines naturally.

## 2. Strong Tidal Rips
During heavy groundswells at Le Moule and Anse Bertrand, immense volumes of water drain through narrow reef passes (*les passes*).
- Never fight a direct rip current heading seaward.
- Paddle parallel to the reef until you reach the shoulder or deep channel where the water settles.

## 3. Tropical Sun & Hydration
The Caribbean UV index routinely hits 11+. Use eco-friendly, reef-safe mineral sunscreen (Zinc Oxide / Titanium Dioxide) and drink plenty of fresh coconut water and electrolytes between sessions.

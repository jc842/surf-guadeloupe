# Surfboard Rentals & Ding Repair Guide in Guadeloupe

Traveling with surfboards can be costly and stressful with airline baggage fees. Guadeloupe boasts a vibrant surf culture with numerous rental hubs and skilled local shapers.

---

## Rental Quivers: What’s Available?
Across Saint-François, Le Moule, and Sainte-Anne, surf shops offer:
- **Softboards (7'0 to 9'0)**: Catch Surf, Softech, and Torq foamies for beginners.
- **Performance Shortboards (5'8 to 6'4)**: PU and epoxy thrusters, fishes, and step-ups for Atlantic swells.
- **Longboards & Mini-Malibus**: 8'0 to 9'6 single-fin and 2+1 setups for mellow peeling waves.
- **Wing Foil & E-Foil Rentals**: Rapidly expanding across Saint-François and Le Gosier lagoons.

## Local Shapers & Ding Repair
Bumping a coral reef at Le Moule is a rite of passage. If your board takes a ding:
- **Fast Turnaround Repairs**: Local surf shops in Saint-François and Le Moule offer 24h to 48h UV resin and epoxy repairs.
- **Custom Shapers**: Consider ordering a custom stick tailored specifically to Caribbean hollow waves.

# Best Surf Schools & Coaching Camps in Guadeloupe

Guadeloupe has bred some of France’s top Olympic and World Tour surfers. Its network of state-certified surf instructors (*BPJEPS & ISA certified*) ensures safe, progressive learning for beginners and advanced competitors alike.

---

## 1. Pôle Espoir & Club de Surf du Moule
The historic cradle of Guadeloupean surfing. While hosting high-performance junior squads, local schools at Plage des Alizés offer structured initiation courses for adults and kids.

## 2. Le Helleux Surf Academies (Sainte-Anne)
Because of its gentle, multi-peak layout, Gros Sable in Sainte-Anne is home to several premier surf clubs:
- **Private & Group Lessons**: Video analysis, pop-up mechanics, and safety briefings on the sand.
- **Equipment Provided**: Soft-top boards, leash, and rash vests.

## 3. Bananier Surf School (Basse-Terre)
For those staying on the lush side of Basse-Terre, Bananier offers ideal conditions to master wave catching without fear of sharp coral reefs.
- **Key Features**: Year-round consistency, gentle volcanic sand, and private coaching.

### What to Expect During Your First Lesson
- **Duration**: Usually 1.5 to 2 hours per session.
- **Ratios**: Max 6 to 8 students per certified instructor.
- **Average Price**: €35 to €50 per group lesson; €80 to €110 for private 1-on-1 coaching.
